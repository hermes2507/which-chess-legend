## Algorithms and references (no code copied)

- Piece-square tables: Tomasz Michniewski, *Simplified Evaluation Function*, Chess Programming Wiki.
- Accuracy formula: adapted from Lichess's published accuracy method (lichess.org/page/accuracy).
- Rhythmogram: after N. P. M. Todd, "The auditory 'primal sketch': a multiscale model of rhythmic grouping", *Journal of New Music Research*, 1994.

- **Famous games:** historical game scores, which are public domain.
