    About

    Which Chess Legend?

    A light-hearted experiment in finding links between character, pace, narrative and chess paraphernalia. You play one game, and the app reads your style from your moves and your clock. It then matches you with a legend, a famous game, and a rhythm.

    ♞
    Created by Santiago Renteria with Claude Code. More experiments at www.renterialab.com

    How it works

    • The bot is a small chess engine written for this project, using alpha-beta search with quiescence. Its random ELO sets how deep it searches, how much randomness it adds, and how often it blunders on purpose.
    • Your style is scored on 15 traits taken from the game: aggression, sacrifices, accuracy, thinking speed, the pace you chose, castling, king walks, early queen moves, pawn storms, trades, game length, opening theory, knights versus bishops, chaos and time trouble.
    • The legends each have a hand-tuned style profile, plus bonuses for well-known quirks (the Bongcloud, the Fischer clock, Bronstein's long first-move thinks).
    • The closest famous game compares your opening moves, style, result and length with 14 historical games.
    • The rhythmogram treats your moves as beats in time and shows how they cluster at every time scale.
